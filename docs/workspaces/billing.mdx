<Shot name="billing" alt="The billing desk: today's takings and the queue of bills on the left, the selected bill in the middle, and the till with cash, card and mobile, a keypad and the change on the right" />

The billing desk is three columns: the queue, the bill, and the till. On a phone they become steps: pick a bill, look it over, pay.

## The queue

The queue reads from the [live store](/how-it-works/live-updates), so bills appear the moment food is served. It has three groups, decided by `billGroup()`:

```ts src/lib/billing.ts
export function billGroup(order: Pick<LiveOrder, "status" | "total" | "balanceDue">) {
  if (order.status === "pending" || order.status === "paid" || order.status === "cancelled") {
    return null;
  }
  if (order.balanceDue <= 0) return "prepaid";
  if (order.status === "served" || order.balanceDue < order.total) return "waiting";
  return "kitchen";
}
```

- **Waiting to pay**: served, or partly paid. Oldest first.
- **Still in the kitchen**: open orders not served yet. Takeaways often pay before the food is ready, so they can be paid from here.
- **Paid today**: quiet rows for reference.

A newly due bill flashes once, and the Billing item in the sidebar carries a count. Above the queue, a strip shows today's sales, paid orders and refunds. The ↑ and ↓ keys move through the queue.

## The till

Pick a method: **Cash**, **Card** or **Mobile**. The keys C, K and M do the same.

- **Cash** asks how much was handed over. Quick buttons offer the exact amount and the next round notes (৳500, ৳1,000), and the change shows in large type at the bottom.
- **Card** takes an optional slip number.
- **Mobile** shows bKash, Nagad and Rocket, and needs the transaction ID. The wallet's name is saved with the payment.

The amount starts at what's left to pay. A keypad sits under it for touch screens; on a laptop you can just type the digits, and Enter takes the payment.

### Splitting a bill

**Whole bill**, **÷2**, **÷3** or **÷4** split what's left evenly. The shares are worked out in paisa, so they always add up to the bill exactly; any odd paisa go to the first payers. After each part payment the next share is filled in, and each part can use a different method, like half cash, half card.

The till checks before sending: there must be an amount, it can't be more than what's left, cash handed over must cover it, and mobile payments need a transaction ID.

### When it's paid

When the balance reaches zero the order closes as **paid**. A green tick draws itself, the change counts up, and the till offers **Print receipt** and **Next bill**. Nothing prints by itself.

<Why>
  The payment button never retries and never pretends. If the network drops halfway, the till re-reads the order from the API instead of guessing, so the cashier sees whether the money was recorded before taking it again. I'd rather show a spinner for two seconds than risk charging a guest twice. More on that in [Data and caching](/how-it-works/data-and-caching).
</Why>

## Receipts

The API makes the receipt itself, as an 80 mm-wide HTML page for thermal printers. The dashboard fetches it with the cashier's token, shows it in a preview (always white, even in dark mode), and prints just that frame. Before payment the button reads **Print bill**; after, **Print receipt**. It's available on the order's own page too.

```tsx src/components/orders/receipt-dialog.tsx
<iframe
  ref={frame}
  title={t("frame")}
  srcDoc={receipt.data}
  sandbox="allow-same-origin allow-modals"
  // The body, not the document: a short receipt would report the frame's own height.
  onLoad={(event) =>
    setHeight(event.currentTarget.contentDocument?.body.scrollHeight ?? null)
  }
  style={{ height: height ?? 480 }}
  className="w-[316px] shrink-0 rounded-sm bg-white shadow-md"
/>
```

The `sandbox` keeps the receipt's HTML from running scripts, and `allow-modals` lets the print dialog open. The frame grows to the receipt's height, so a long bill isn't cut off.

## Refunds

Managers get **Refund** on the bill and on the order's page. The form takes an amount (with an "All" shortcut), the method (defaulting to how it was paid), an optional reference and a reason with quick picks. A confirmation step follows before anything is sent. A refund never makes a paid order unpaid; it's recorded against the order and shows under refunds in today's takings and the reports.

To cancel an order that has taken money, refund it first. The cancel dialog says so and links straight to the refund.
